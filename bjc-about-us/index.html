Who are we?

A home where all are welcome no matter background or affiliation.

A place where every Jew is family.

A Jewish organization in the Greater Bronx that provides educational, social, and recreational programming for families, seniors and businessmen alike. 

A space where one can socialize in a comfortable home-like setting with great friends, superb food and stimulating discussion.

A forum where you can question your faith and not be afraid of judgment.

A haven to run to when you're stressed or lonely and need a true friend. 

Our Mission

Our goal is to build a warm, thriving community, as only Chabad can, with weekly classes, community Shabbos meals, programs for young professionals, seniors, The Newly launched Atid academy of the Bronx a much needed Jewish preschool, and most importantly, a home for Bronx Jews.

A center for prayer and study, where you can gain a deeper understanding and appreciation of your Jewish heritage.

A partnership between community members to help create programs, plan social action projects, promote health awareness activities and offer many volunteer opportunities.

An academy providing texts and teachers for those who seek to learn.

An environment where being Jewish is fun!

Come join and experience it for yourself!
